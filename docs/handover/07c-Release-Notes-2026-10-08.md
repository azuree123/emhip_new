# EMHIP Release Notes: 8 October 2026

_Version 1.0 · 8 October 2026 · For: the customer project team, testers, Hub Managers and administrators_

This release answers the customer's round 3 feedback of 8 October 2026. It adds a "How did you hear about us?" question to registration, lets one activity or hospitality contact be logged for a whole group, asks for the action taken when an urgent case is resolved, adds an Urgent Case History tab to the guest record, shows the contact type instead of the contact method on the guest record, and makes every figure on the Contact History screen open the contacts behind it. One database migration runs automatically when the release is deployed. There are no new settings or permissions.

## At a glance

| Area | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | "How did you hear about us?" tickboxes on the first page, with a box for Other |
| Contact logging | **Group Contact** logs one Activity or Hospitality contact for up to 60 guests at once |
| Urgent cases | **Mark as resolved** asks for the action taken; a new **Urgent Case History** tab on the guest record |
| Guest record | Contact History and the Overview's recent activity lead with the contact type |
| Contact History screen | Every tile and CPN figure opens its list of contacts; the CPN section has its own time filter; My caseload includes a CPN's own guests |

## For administrators

- Migration `GuestHeardAboutUs` adds two columns to Guests. Existing guests read "not recorded". It is applied automatically on deployment.
- New endpoints: `POST /guests/group-contacts`, `GET /urgent-cases/{guestId}/history`, `GET /contacts/list` and `GET /reports/heard-about-us`. The Contact History endpoints accept an optional `caseload` parameter.
- `POST /urgent-cases/episodes/{id}/resolve` now requires `resolutionNote`.

## Known limits

- The CPN home dashboard has not been changed: its caseload counts may still be based only on the guest's CMHW. This has not been checked yet.
- Within a CPN's caseload, the main Contact History tiles count all contacts with those guests, including the CMHW's, not only the CPN's own. The CPN activity section counts only CPN contacts.
- Contacts recorded from **Scheduled contacts → Record contact** still have no contact type. Contacts logged with Add Contact or Group Contact always do.
