Custom fields

Extra questions of your own, added to the forms below. They appear in an Additional information panel at the bottom of the matching form, under the standard fields. The standardised clinical instruments — DIALOG scores and the risk assessment — are intentionally not extensible, so their wording and scoring stay comparable across guests and over time.
